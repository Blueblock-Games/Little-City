# Little City

**Build Your City. Shape Your World.**

Little City is a fun, creative city-building game developed by **Blueblock Games**.

Build houses, roads, schools, hospitals, parks, and businesses. Earn money, expand your city, and turn a small town into a thriving metropolis!

## Features

- Build and customize your own city.
- Earn money and expand over time.
- Construct buildings and essential services.
- Choose when to play the background music and adjust its volume.
- Watch the city cycle through five minutes of day and two minutes of night, or switch the time manually. Nighttime plays its own music.
- Invite friends to build together in multiplayer.
- Play on desktop and mobile.

## How to Play

1. Name your city.
2. Start building houses and roads.
3. Earn money and unlock new possibilities.
4. Expand your city and build with friends!
