How to Play Little City

Ready to become the mayor? Here's how to get started.

  1. Name your city. Choose a name for your new world.
  2. Start building. Place roads, houses, and other buildings.
  3. Earn money. Your city generates income over time.
  4. Keep expanding. Add schools, hospitals, parks, and services.
  5. Play together. Use multiplayer features to collaborate with friends.
